# Lore — the slice the game needs

This is a working subset of the setting, written for people designing missions, boards, abilities and pilots. It is **not canon**. Canon lives in Froglet's own lore material; if this file disagrees with it, canon wins — tell Garrett and fix this file.

This repository is public. Keep this file to what the game needs in order to be designed. Unreleased story, named canon characters, and anything internal do not belong here.

## Tumult

Tumult is a built world, far larger than Earth — roughly ten times the surface — and on it gravity is a geography. Near the poles it is crushing, around ten times Earth's; near the equator it is about a tenth of Earth's. People say **uphill** for toward the heavy poles and **downhill** for toward the light equator, whatever the ground is doing.

A day lasts about an hour. Night is never far away, and never long.

The wind is strong and constant enough that **windbreaks** are infrastructure. The ground itself is not permanent: terrain on Tumult deforms, collapses and is rebuilt.

It is a resource-rich world, and very large creatures live there too.

## People and clades

Every living thing on Tumult is a triple hybrid. People always include *human* plus two other **clades**. A person is described by their two clades (jerboa and barn owl; octopus and grasshopper).

When drawing or describing people: two clades *expressed in a humanoid* — build, markings, eyes, hands, texture, habits. Not animal heads on human bodies.

In the game, each clade contributes nine traits. A character holds at most three traits from any one clade.

## Three ways to get things done

- **Adaptation** — what a body can do. Reliable; costs stamina. In the game: the Physical face of a trait, paid in action points.
- **Magic** — etherions. Powerful, and addictive. In the game: the Ethereal face, paid in etherion dice.
- **Technology** — capable and resource-hungry. In the game: gear, fuelled by omnicrystals.

## Etherions

A class of particle that condenses into four kinds of crystal — **Mass**, **Charge**, **Space** and **Time** — and into **omnicrystals**, which can stand in for any of them and are what people trade with.

| Etherion | Its surface in play |
|---|---|
| Mass | bodies and force — push, pull, weight, anchoring |
| Charge | energy and links — shock, fields, chains, sensing |
| Space | position and shape — reach, area, swapping, height |
| Time | tempo — haste, delay, stasis, foresight, recovery |

## Domains

Three **domains** describe how people choose to live and work. They are not factions, sides or teams, and a person's domain can change.

| Domain | Values | Typical work | Rewarded with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jade** | order, community | protect | fame, standing |
| **Ruby** | freedom, self-reliance | explore | notoriety |
| **Gold** | profit, commerce | escort, contract | money |

On Tumult everyone begins Ruby. Taking a paid contract moves you toward Gold; accepting a volunteer appointment moves you toward Jade.

## Beacons and rescue

**Beacons** are waypoints and safe ground. A beacon belongs to a domain and refuels those who share it.

When a party gets into trouble, how it is rescued depends on its domain: under Jade, rescue is free; under Gold, rescue comes and is charged for; under Ruby, rescue has to have been arranged with a deposit beforehand.

## Guides

Travel across Tumult is led by **guides**. There are guide services by land, by sea and by air. The fourth way onward is to leave: to join the Cosmic Shore.

## The Terminal

A presence throughout the setting that appears only as an interface — prompts, readouts, holographic terminals. It is never a character in a scene. In the game it is the voice of briefings and logs.

## Beyond Tumult

Beyond Tumult is the **Hypersea**, where pilots fly vessels from great **Arks**. A **squad** is three pilots in three vessels; a crew is everyone flying from one Ark. The game's proposed campaign ends where that life begins ([design/01](design/01-frame-and-campaign.md)), so little of it needs to appear on the board — but it is where a finished dossier is headed.

## Words to use

- **Ark** — the only name for the great carrier vessels.
- **Domain** — never "faction" for Jade, Ruby and Gold.
- **Squad** — three pilots. Do not write "crew of three". (The game fields two heroes today; a squad of three is proposed.)
- **Clade** — the code still says `species` and the v2.0 interface says "bloodline"; both will be renamed together.
- **Uphill / downhill** — toward heavy / toward light.
- **Pilot** — what squad members become. On Tumult, during a season, they are **guides**.

## What the game invents, and what it must not

Missions, patrons, places and creatures in the game are generic unless the lore team supplies names. The game may invent a nameless "salvage contract at a broken span"; it may not invent history, name canon people, or decide how the larger story goes.
