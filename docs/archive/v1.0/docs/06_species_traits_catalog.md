# Tumult — Species Trait Catalog (v0.1)

*A character-building substrate for the HyperSea cast of **Cosmic Shore**. Every person of Tumult is humanoid to first approximation because **Human** is always one of their three species. Players choose the other two from this list, then draft from all three.*

**Drafting & growth.** Each species offers **9 traits**. At creation you take **2 of the 9** from each of your three species — a **6-trait** starting character that is only **2/9 of each heritage**, deliberately partial and still-becoming. Advancement is a three-way tension: deepen a trait you hold (raise its **P** or **E** track), unlock its **third face** (X = 2 → 3), **or** claim a wholly new trait in one of your species — up to a hard ceiling of **3 of 9 per species (9 total)**. No one ever completes a species, so every character is one specific *reading* of its three bloodlines. With 84 possible trait-triads per species, no two same-heritage characters play alike.

> Design consequence: because the lists are now **draft pools**, balance is judged per-*trait* — every entry should be worth a scarce 1-of-3 slot, not just pleasant texture.

---

## How to read a card

Every trait is a card with **two faces** at creation (X = 2): a **Physical** action paid in **Action Points (AP)**, and a **Magical** action paid in **Etherions** of one type. Advancement lets you **unlock a third face** (X = 3) instead of only deepening a track — breadth vs. depth, your call.

**The four etherions**
- **◆M Mass** — force, weight, momentum, density, inertia
- **◆C Charge** — electricity, magnetism, light, fields, chemical bonds
- **◆S Space** — distance, position, folding, teleportation, area
- **◆T Time** — speed, delay, duration, regeneration, foresight

**Tracks.** Each card has a **Physical level (P)** and an **Ethereal level (E)**. Effects scale per level as noted. Per the jumping precedent: **a card's Magical face also inherits its Physical level's magnitude bonuses**, so investing physically deepens *both* faces — pure-ethereal builds are cheaper but flatter, hybrid builds hit hardest.

**Units.** *Zone* = one spatial band (reskin to HyperSea currents/lanes as needed). *Round* = one turn cycle. ★ marks the 1–2 signature traits that make a species itself.

> **System invariant — no turn theft.** No ability removes, skips, or fully controls another player's turn. Control may *tax* efficiency (extra cost, −1/−2 penalties, movement-only immobilize) but never strips agency, and may never chain (even via cooldown/track manipulation) into an effective skip. Cordyceps Behavioral Hijack compels a *single simple move*, never a whole turn or a self-/ally-harming action. Self-imposed stasis (Tardigrade Cryptobiosis, Flytrap Dormancy) is fine — it's your choice on your turn.

# 0 · Human  *(fixed — every character carries this third)*
*Homo sapiens — no fang, no venom, no wall-climbing setae. What's specifically human is the cognitive and social stack: a brain that models other minds, hands that build, a body tuned for relentless endurance rather than burst, and language that turns a crowd into a single organism. Where the other species hand you a weapon, Human hands you leverage.*
**Ladder:** species → Homininae → Primates → Mammalia → Vertebrata

**0.1 Theory of Mind** ★ · *species*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Model a creature's intent — predict whether it will attack, flee, or bluff this round; gain +1 against it accordingly. *+1 read or +1 bonus per P.*
◆T **Time** (2): Run the simulation forward — declare an enemy's next action before it does; if right, pre-empt it. *+1 modeled target per E.*

**0.2 Tool Use** ★ · *Homininae*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Improvise a tool or weapon from the environment, gaining a relevant bonus until it breaks. *Tool quality/durability +1 per P.*
◆S **Space** (2): Assemble a device that folds space — a grapple-line, a thrown anchor, a reach-extender — letting you act on a target 1 zone beyond normal. *+1 reach/use per E.*

**0.3 Persistence Endurance** · *Homininae (persistence hunting)*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Pace yourself — ignore fatigue and keep a steady move every round of a chase or march; outlast faster sprinters. *+1 sustained round per P.*
◆T **Time** (2): Stretch your stamina clock — convert a burst of exertion into many even rounds, never tiring first. *+1 round per 2 E.*

**0.4 Throwing Arm** · *Homininae*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Hurl an object or weapon accurately up to 2 zones — the one ranged attack no other species does naturally. *+1 zone or +1 dmg per P.*
◆M **Mass** (2): Load the throw with momentum — the projectile knocks back or pins what it hits. *Force/knockback +1 per E.*

**0.5 Language** ★(minor) · *species*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Coordinate allies with speech — one ally in earshot may immediately take a free reaction. *+1 coordinated ally per P.*
◆C **Charge** (2): Charge words with conviction — a rallying call or command that buffs every ally (or rattles every foe) in a zone. *Radius/effect +1 per E.*

**0.6 Pattern Recognition** · *Primates*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Spot the trick — reveal a hidden trap, lie, or mechanism you have a moment to study. *+1 detail tier per P.*
◆S **Space** (1): Map the system at a glance — perceive the structure of a puzzle, network, or battlefield as a whole and find its weak point. *+1 scope per E.*

**0.7 Opposable Grip** · *Primates*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Precise manipulation — pick a lock, disarm a device, or wield a complex tool with fine control. *+1 finesse tier per P.*
◆M **Mass** (1): Apply leverage — multiply your effective force on one held object or joint, far past your size. *Force multiplier +1 per E.*

**0.8 Cooperative Instinct** · *Homininae*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Set up an ally — grant the next attack or action they take against a shared target +2. *+1 assist per P.*
◆C **Charge** (2): Forge a shared field of trust — networked allies pool a bonus and act as one coordinated unit this round. *+1 pooled ally/bonus per E.*

**0.9 Adaptive Generalist** · *species*
⟡ **Physical** (1 AP): Jack-of-all-trades — attempt any action with no untrained penalty once this scene. *+1 use per 2 P.*
◆T **Time** (2): Learn on the fly — after seeing any action this scene, briefly replicate its basic form at low effect. *Replicated level +1 per E.*

## Designer notes

- **Etherion spread (99 cards across 11 species):** still close to even — each etherion sits roughly in the low-to-mid 20s — so no single type is a tax every build pays. Human leans **Time** (foresight, endurance, learning) and **Charge** (the social field of language and cooperation), which is exactly *right*: humanity's edge in this bestiary is cognitive-temporal and social, not physical. Each non-human species keeps its own color leaning (Eel → Charge, Tardigrade → Time/Space, Flytrap → a Charge resource-engine) so heritages stay distinct without becoming etherion monocultures.
- **The draft changes what "balance" means.** With only **2-of-9 at start and 3-of-9 at cap**, each trait competes for a scarce slot, so the bar is "worth a 1-of-3 commitment," not "nice texture." Re-scan the supporting traits I wrote as connective tissue under the old all-9 model — Jerboa Cheek Caching, Owl Threat Display, Octopus Chemo-Suckers, a few others — and decide whether to buff them into slot-worthy picks or leave them as the deliberately humble options that reward synergy rather than standing alone. Human's list was written *post*-change, so all nine are meant to hold their own as a 1-of-3 pick.
- **Overlap audit (you asked me to fight it):** all entries are mechanically distinct. The closest neighbors are intentional and play differently: *grips* (owl Talon = pin, gecko Lockjaw = won't-release crush, tardigrade Clawed = immovable mutual hold, flytrap Snap = counted trigger, human Opposable Grip = leverage/finesse); *foresight* (owl Binocular = initiative, octopus Adaptive = counter one action, human Theory of Mind = read *intent* + pre-empt) — different etherions, costs, and timing. The jerboa+grasshopper "jumping" precedent is the one place I deliberately kept a shared theme but split the mechanic (Space fold-hop vs. Mass charge-slam).
- **Resource engines:** Flytrap Photosynthesis and Gecko Solar Basking generate AP/etherions; Grasshopper Voracious Mandibles, Cordyceps Radiotrophic Resilience, Tardigrade Stylet Drain, and Human Cooperative Instinct convert/route them. Under the 3-of-9 cap an "economist" build now has to *spend three precious slots* to assemble a self-sustaining loop — the draft ceiling is itself the balance lever.
- **For the HyperSea cast specifically:** Cordyceps (Behavioral Hijack, networked), Mimic Octopus (Impersonation), and Tardigrade (void traversal — perfect for a space setting) read as natural antagonist/wildcard kits. Human's stack — Theory of Mind, Language, Cooperative Instinct — is what makes a Tumult NPC feel *socially* dangerous rather than just physically. The Axolotl's cancer/degeneration resistance is a quiet nod you'll recognize.
- **Reskin hooks:** "zone" → HyperSea current/lane; AP → your action-point economy; the four etherions already map to Cosmic Shore's physics framing (Mass/Charge/Space/Time), so trail-prism and vessel-class flavor ride straight on top.
